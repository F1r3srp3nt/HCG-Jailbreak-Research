## 0.2.0~beta1

Free experimental beta. Not yet installed or tested on a device.

- **New: Tornado.** As you swipe between Home Screen pages, the icons lift out of the grid into a spinning 3-D tornado
  funnel that travels with your finger, and the next page's icons unwind into place. Pause, reverse or let go and the
  icons return exactly. Controls: intensity, spiral radius, spiral turns, spin direction, depth & scale, and optional
  icon spin (off by default, so icons stay readable). Live preview in Settings. Uses the stock animation when Reduce
  Motion is on.
- Reverse Slide, Vertical Rise, Vertical Fall and Custom Directions are unchanged.
- Packaged with gzip, like 0.1.0~beta2.
