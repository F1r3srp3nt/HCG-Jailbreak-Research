1

Basics

What is jailbreaking?

An iPhone only runs software that Apple has approved and signed, and every app is kept inside its own sandbox, a fenced-off area it cannot leave. Those rules are a large part of why iOS is secure.

Jailbreaking uses security flaws in iOS (exploits) to lift some of those rules on your own device. Once jailbroken, you can install software Apple would not allow, change how iOS looks and behaves, and get deeper access to the file system. ios.cfw.guide sums up what you get: “Gain full access to your device's OS”, a package manager such as Sileo or Zebra, themes, and tweaks that “change how iOS behaves”.

How a modern jailbreak runs

Dopamine is semi-untethered: it “requires an app to re-apply the exploit after a reboot”. The jailbreak does not survive a full restart. After every restart your device is back to normal iOS until you open the Dopamine app and tap Jailbreak again. A respring (restarting only the Home Screen) or a userspace reboot keeps you jailbroken.

Is it legal?

ios.cfw.guide notes that in the United States the Copyright Office has granted an exemption for jailbreaking since 2010, and advises you to “check in your country whether it is legal there”. Legal is not the same as supported: Apple says unauthorized modification “is a violation of the iOS and iPadOS Software License Agreement” and that it “may deny service” for a device running unauthorized software (Apple's statement as published on the official Dopamine website). Chapter 4 covers what that means in practice.

Sources: ios.cfw.guide: Types of Jailbreak, FAQ, ellekit.space/dopamine.

2

Motivation

Why do people jailbreak?

  • Personalisation. Themes, Home Screen and Lock Screen layouts, animations and system behaviour that iOS settings do not offer.
  • Features Apple does not provide. Tweaks add controls, shortcuts, gestures and options to existing apps and to iOS itself.
  • Learning and development. A terminal, the file system and developer tools on the device are useful for students, developers and security researchers studying their own hardware.
  • Ownership. Some people simply want full control over a device they paid for.
Do you actually need it?

If you only want a feature Shortcuts, Focus modes, widgets or an App Store app can provide, use those: they keep every iOS protection. Jailbreak because you understand the trade-offs in Chapters 3 and 4, not because a video made it look easy.

3

Trade-offs

Benefits and limitations

Benefits

  • Tweaks, themes and command-line tools from package managers.
  • Deeper file-system access (within the rootless limits of Chapter 5).
  • Dopamine is open source and removable from its own app.
  • Semi-untethered: a normal restart always gives you stock iOS back, which makes many problems easy to escape.

Limitations

  • You are frozen on old iOS. Dopamine supports specific versions only; updating usually ends your ability to jailbreak.
  • You must re-run Dopamine after every full restart.
  • On the sideloading route the Dopamine app must be re-signed every 7 days.
  • Tweaks must be built for rootless jailbreaks and for your iOS version; many older tweaks are not.
  • Banking, payment, some games and other apps may refuse to run.
  • Tweaks can make the device unstable and drain the battery.

Sources: ios.cfw.guide: Installing Dopamine, ellekit.space/dopamine, Blocking Jailbreak Detection.

4

Read before you decide

Security, privacy, stability and warranty risks

The official Dopamine website opens with Apple's warning, quoted here because it is the fairest summary of the downside:

“Jailbreaking your device eliminates security layers designed to protect your personal information and your iOS device. With this security removed from your iPhone, hackers may steal your personal information, damage your device, attack your network, or introduce malware, spyware, or viruses.”

Apple, as published on ellekit.space/dopamine.

Security

  • Weakened protections. The jailbreak works by bypassing kernel protections (Dopamine's log shows steps such as “Exploiting Kernel”, “Bypassing PAC” and “Bypassing PPL”). Code that iOS would block can now run with elevated privileges.
  • Old, unpatched iOS. To stay jailbreakable you stay on a version with publicly known vulnerabilities. Every security fix Apple ships later is one you do not get. This is the biggest long-term cost.
  • Update blocking has a price. ios.cfw.guide documents a profile that stops iOS updates so you are not updated by accident. Installing it also stops security updates, and it is a configuration profile from a third party. Only install it if you accept both; remove it when you stop jailbreaking.
  • Accounts on the device. Dopamine lets you set the password of the mobile user, which “can also be used for root access using sudo”. If you enable SSH or a developer shell, set a strong password first.

Privacy

  • A tweak can be loaded into almost any process, including Messages, Mail and your password manager. Install only what you trust, from sources you trust (Chapter 12).
  • Malicious or pirated packages are a classic way to spread spyware on jailbroken devices.

Stability and data

  • Apple lists “frequent and unexpected crashes”, “shortened battery life”, “unreliable voice and data” and disrupted services such as iCloud, iMessage, FaceTime and Apple Pay.
  • An incompatible tweak can cause a respring loop. ios.cfw.guide warns jailbreaking “can lead to data loss if you damage the OS”.
  • If your phone has a replacement screen, ios.cfw.guide warns its touchscreen may stop working after the jailbreak's userspace reboot.

Banking and other apps

Many apps detect jailbreaks and refuse to run, including banking apps and some online games. ios.cfw.guide's own advice: “We do not recommend using banking apps on a jailbroken device. Use a separate secure device to access your bank account.” Dopamine's Hide Jailbreak option only removes jailbreak files temporarily and, in Dopamine's words, “is not entirely effective and does not conceal everything”. This guide does not cover defeating app security checks.

Warranty and service

Apple “may deny service for an iPhone that has any unauthorized software installed”. ios.cfw.guide adds that your warranty is void “until it is restored to an un-jailbroken state, which is easy to do”. Remove the jailbreak (Chapter 14) before any repair or service appointment.

Sources: ellekit.space/dopamine; ios.cfw.guide FAQ; Blocking Updates; Blocking Jailbreak Detection; Dopamine 3.0.13 app strings (source).

Concepts

Rootless versus rootful jailbreaking

Older jailbreaks were rootful: they wrote their files into the system part of the disk, the root (/). Dopamine is rootless. In the words of its website, rootless jailbreaks “do not provide full access to the iOS device's operating system, as only certain parts of the filesystem can be written to.”

Package layouts
LayoutPackage architectureFiles installed underExamples
Rootlessiphoneos-arm64/var/jb/… onlyDopamine, palera1n (rootless)
Rootfuliphoneos-arm/palera1n (rootful), older jailbreaks
Roothideiphoneos-arm64ea randomised jailbreak rootseparate tools (not covered here)

What it means for you

  • Install only tweaks built for rootless. Dopamine's website: “Users should check if their tweaks are compatible with iOS 15+ [and] are properly compiled for rootless before installing them.”
  • Sileo only lists packages whose architecture matches your device, so most incompatible rootful packages are simply hidden.
  • Developers: rebuild for rootless (Theos THEOS_PACKAGE_SCHEME = rootless) and resolve paths at run time with libroot rather than hard-coding /var/jb.

Sources: ellekit.space/dopamine; HCG Repo docs/ROOTLESS_ROOTFUL.md (Theos rootless guide, Procursus, Sileo source review).

Before you start

Preparing your iPhone and backing up

ios.cfw.guide “strongly” recommends a backup and says that “if no issues occur during installation, you will be jailbroken without any data loss”. Prepare for the case where issues do occur.

Preparation checklist

0 of 10 preparation items done.

What a backup can and cannot do

  • A backup restores your data. It does not restore your iOS version or your jailbreak.
  • Restoring the phone with a computer installs a version Apple still signs, normally the newest. That usually ends jailbreak support for good, and you cannot choose to go back to an older version.
  • A backup made on a newer iOS version cannot be restored to a device running an older one.
  • Keep a backup from before you jailbreak. Backups made while jailbroken can carry tweak settings and leftovers back to a clean phone.

Installing Dopamine

Preparation not finished

Your checklist in Chapter 8 is not complete. You can read on, but finish it before you change anything on your device.

These steps summarise the official ios.cfw.guide instructions for Dopamine. Follow the official page side by side: if it differs from this summary, it wins. HCG has no way to install anything on your device.

Get the official Dopamine package

  • Download Dopamine.ipa only from ellekit.space/dopamine (“Download .ipa”, or “Open in TrollStore”) or from github.com/opa334/Dopamine/releases.
  • Do not use copies from mirrors, “signing services”, video descriptions, Discord files or HCG: HCG does not host Dopamine. A modified jailbreak runs with full control of your phone.

Then follow the route the checker gave you. Use the tabs below (arrow keys switch tabs).

TrollStore permanently installs Dopamine, so it never expires. TrollStore itself can only run on 14.0 beta 2 – 16.6.1, 16.7 RC (20H18) and 17.0 (TrollStore README).

The method depends on your chip and version. The checker names your method; the official page has a tab for each:

Follow your method's tab on Installing Dopamine (TrollStore), including Install Persistence Helper. TrollStore's README explains why: when iOS reloads its icon cache, TrollStore apps stop launching until the persistence helper re-registers them.

Part B. Install Dopamine with TrollStore

  1. Save the .ipa on the device. Download Dopamine.ipa from the official site in Safari (or tap “Open in TrollStore” there).
  2. Open TrollStore and tap + at the top right.
  3. Choose the file. Go to where you saved the Dopamine .ipa and tap it.
  4. Tap Install. Dopamine appears on your Home Screen. No trust step and no Developer Mode are needed on this route.

For 16.7 – 16.7.x (not 16.7 RC) and 17.0.1 or later, where TrollStore cannot run. The app is signed with your Apple Account and must be re-signed every 7 days. This route does not work on the iOS version a device originally shipped with.

No computer?

ios.cfw.guide says the computer is only needed “unless jailbreaks.app is signed”. When we reviewed it, the guide said jailbreaks.app was signing Dopamine 2.5b4, an older version than 3.0.13. Signing status changes often; check which version you are getting.

You need

  • A Windows, macOS or Linux computer and a cable.
  • PlumeImpactor, the latest release from its official GitHub page, and iTunes if you use Windows.
  • The official Dopamine.ipa on the computer.
  • Your Apple Account. PlumeImpactor uses it to sign the app. Only enter it into the official PlumeImpactor build; some people use a separate Apple Account for sideloading.
  1. Connect and trust. Plug the device into the computer, and make sure the computer is trusted and allowed to view its contents.
  2. Sign in to PlumeImpactor. Open it, go to Settings › Sign In, enter your Apple Account and password, then close the Settings and Sign In windows.
  3. Install. Drag and drop the Dopamine .ipa into PlumeImpactor and click Install. After this, the computer is no longer needed.
  4. Trust the app. On the device go to Settings › General › Device Management (or VPN & Device Management / Profiles and Device Management) › your Apple Account › Trust.
  5. Turn on Developer Mode (iOS 16 or later). If you did not use jailbreaks.app: Settings › Privacy & Security › Developer Mode, switch it on and follow the on-screen instructions (the device restarts).
Every 7 days

Apps signed with a regular Apple Account expire after 7 days (365 days with a paid Apple Developer account). After that Dopamine will not open, so you cannot re-jailbreak after a restart until you re-sign it. See ios.cfw.guide: Sideloading Apps. Already-installed tweaks keep their files; they just are not active while the device is not jailbroken.

Installation

Running the jailbreak

  1. Reboot the device. Not required, but recommended by the official guide.
  2. Open Dopamine from the Home Screen immediately afterwards.
  3. Tap Jailbreak. Do not touch the device while it works.
    • On some 2 GB RAM devices on 15.8.7 – 15.8.8 or 16.7 and later, Dopamine first asks for a respring (Respring Required › Apply Workaround). After the respring, open Dopamine and tap Jailbreak again. On A8 devices this may need several tries.
    • On A12/A13 devices on 16.6 or later the screen briefly turns off and on. That is expected.
  4. Choose a package manager (first run). Dopamine asks you to Select Package Manager(s): “If you are unsure which one to select, select Sileo”. You can choose both.
  5. Wait for the userspace reboot. The screen goes dark and the Lock Screen returns. Dopamine then shows Jailbroken, and Sileo and/or Zebra are on your Home Screen.
If it fails

“If the app or your device crashes/restarts unexpectedly and the jailbreak isn't installed, simply try rebooting and running the exploit again until it does work.” (ios.cfw.guide). Exploits are not 100% reliable.

First-time setup: ElleKit and PreferenceLoader

The official guide then updates and installs two essentials in Sileo (Chapter 11 explains the app):

  1. ElleKit. Sources tab › ElleKit repository › All Categories › ElleKit › Get.
  2. PreferenceLoader. Search tab › search PreferenceLoader › Get. It lets tweaks add their settings to the Settings app.
  3. Confirm. Tap the Queued bar at the bottom, then Confirm.
  4. Tap Reboot Device when it finishes. Despite the name this is not a full reboot: you stay jailbroken.

On 15.0 – 16.6.1, 16.7 RC and 17.0, if you sideloaded Dopamine, the official guide then recommends installing TrollStore through Sileo's TrollStore Helper and reinstalling Dopamine with it, so you never have to re-sign. Follow “Next Steps” on Installing Dopamine.

How to tell it worked

  • Dopamine shows Jailbroken.
  • Sileo (or Zebra) opens and refreshes its sources without errors.
  • ElleKit and PreferenceLoader appear under Sileo's installed packages.

What happens after a reboot

  • After a full restart the device starts as normal iOS: no tweaks run. Your jailbreak files and installed packages stay on the device.
  • To jailbreak again: open Dopamine, tap Jailbreak. On the sideload route this only works while the app's 7-day signature is valid.
  • A respring (Restart SpringBoard) or userspace reboot (Reboot Userspace, or Sileo's Reboot Device) keeps you jailbroken.
  • When Dopamine offers an update, it shows Update Available inside the app.

Sources: ios.cfw.guide Installing Dopamine (“Running Dopamine”, “Installing necessary software”, “Next Steps”); Dopamine 3.0.13 app strings.

11

Package managers

Installing and using Sileo or Zebra

A package manager is the jailbreak's App Store: it installs, updates and removes tweaks, themes and tools. ios.cfw.guide: they “can be considered similar to the iOS App Store, where you can install both free and paid software.” Dopamine offers two:

Sileo

Dopamine's suggestion if you are unsure, and the one the official setup steps use. Credited on the Dopamine website to Amy While.

Zebra

An alternative package manager. Credited on the Dopamine website to Adam Demasi. It works with the same sources and packages.

Chose only one at first? Install the other later with Dopamine › Settings › Reinstall Package Managers. Use one at a time for changes, so two apps do not edit the same package database simultaneously.

Everyday tasks

  • Add a source: Sources tab › +, enter the URL exactly (with the trailing slash), let it refresh. Many sites have an “Add to Sileo” or “Add to Zebra” button that fills it in for you.
  • Find a package: Search tab, or browse a source's categories.
  • Install: open the package, read its description, compatibility and dependencies, tap Get, then the Queued bar › Confirm.
  • Update: pull down to refresh sources, then review the available updates. Read changelogs; update a few things at a time.
  • Remove: find the package among your installed packages, open it and choose Remove, then confirm. Respring if asked.

Command line (optional)

With a terminal app or SSH, the same database is managed by APT. For example, after adding a source file:

sudo apt update

Commands are shown for you to copy and run yourself; nothing runs from this page.

Concepts

Understanding repositories and packages

A repository (repo, or source) is a website that lists packages. ios.cfw.guide: repos “are essentially databases that contain lots of tweaks for you to install.” A package is a .deb file containing a tweak, theme, app or tool, plus a description of what it needs.

Parts of a package listing
Package IDA unique name, e.g. com.hakmicapitalgroup.hcgnexus.
Version~beta in a version marks a pre-release.
Architectureiphoneos-arm64 = rootless (what Dopamine uses). iphoneos-arm = rootful.
DependsOther packages it needs, often preferenceloader and an injector (mobilesubstrate or ellekit), and sometimes a minimum firmware (iOS) version.
DepictionThe package's information page: features, compatibility, known issues, changelog.

Staying safe with sources

Malicious repositories
  • A package installs with the highest privileges on the device. Add only sources you have a reason to trust, and remove ones you no longer use.
  • Avoid “cracked” or pirated tweak repos. They are a well-known route for malware, and they take income from developers.
  • Never turn off APT signature checking to make a source work.
  • Be wary of any source or tweak that asks for your Apple Account, banking or other passwords.

Avoiding incompatible tweaks

  • Read the depiction: is it built for rootless (iphoneos-arm64) and does it list your iOS version?
  • Install one tweak at a time and use your device for a bit before adding the next. When something breaks, you know which one did it.
  • Two tweaks that change the same part of iOS (for example two Lock Screen tweaks) often conflict.
  • “Builds and installs” is not the same as “tested on a device like mine”. Prefer packages whose depiction lists tested devices.

Example: adding HCG Repo

HCG Repo is one example of a source. Adding it is optional and not needed for Dopamine.

https://repo.hakmicapitalgroup.com/repo/

Or, from a terminal, as a file under /var/jb/etc/apt/sources.list.d/ followed by sudo apt update:

deb https://repo.hakmicapitalgroup.com/repo/ ./

When things go wrong

Safe Mode, troubleshooting and recovery

Know your restarts

Kinds of restart and whether you stay jailbroken
ActionWhat restartsStill jailbroken?
RespringOnly SpringBoard (Home Screen and system interface)Yes
Userspace rebootEverything above the kernelYes
Full reboot / force restartThe whole deviceNo: stock iOS until you run Dopamine

Safe Mode

In Safe Mode the device is jailbroken but no tweaks are loaded, so you can still open Sileo or Zebra and remove the package causing trouble. Dopamine has two ways into it:

  • You choose it: Dopamine › Settings › Tweak Injection off, then accept the userspace reboot.
  • Dopamine chooses it: if iOS hits a userspace panic, Dopamine performs a userspace reboot instead of a full panic, saves a “userspace-panic” log, and turns tweak injection off. If your tweaks suddenly stopped working after a crash, look at Tweak Injection.

When SpringBoard itself keeps crashing, the system can also come up in a Safe Mode screen with tweaks unloaded. Do not just tap to exit: first remove or disable the tweak you installed or changed last, or SpringBoard may load it again.

The universal escape

Because Dopamine is semi-untethered, a force restart always brings you back to normal iOS with no tweaks running. iPhone 8 or later: press and release volume up, press and release volume down, then hold the side button until the Apple logo appears. iPhone 7: hold volume down and the side button. iPhone 6s and SE (1st generation): hold the Home and side (or top) buttons. For iPads, see Apple's “Force restart iPad”.

Troubleshooting

Dopamine crashes, or the device restarts during the jailbreak

Normal for exploits now and then. Reboot, open Dopamine immediately, and tap Jailbreak again. Repeat if needed (ios.cfw.guide).

Dopamine says “Respring Required” before it will jailbreak

Some 2 GB RAM devices need a workaround first. Tap Apply Workaround; after the respring, open Dopamine and tap Jailbreak. On A8 devices it may need several attempts.

Respring loop after installing or changing a tweak
  1. Force restart (see above). You boot into stock iOS.
  2. Open Dopamine › Settings › turn Tweak Injection off, then jailbreak.
  3. Open Sileo or Zebra and remove the tweak you installed or changed last.
  4. Turn Tweak Injection back on and accept the userspace reboot.

ios.cfw.guide: “the most likely culprit is your most recently installed/updated tweak”.

All my tweaks stopped working after a crash

Dopamine turns tweak injection off after it catches a userspace panic. Check Dopamine › Settings › Tweak Injection. Before switching it back on, remove whatever caused the crash.

The Dopamine app will not open (sideload route)

Its 7-day signature has probably expired. Re-sign it with PlumeImpactor (ios.cfw.guide: Resigning Apps), then jailbreak. On 15.0 – 16.6.1, 16.7 RC and 17.0, consider moving to the TrollStore route.

TrollStore or apps installed with it will not open

When iOS reloads its icon cache, TrollStore apps revert to a non-launchable state. Open your persistence helper app (often Tips) and tap Refresh App Registrations.

Sileo, Zebra or other jailbreak app icons are missing

Use Dopamine › Settings › Refresh Jailbreak Apps. From a terminal you can also rebuild the icon cache:

uicache -a
Dopamine reports duplicate apps and cannot continue

An app that Dopamine ships (for example a package manager) is also installed separately, or registered under a different path. Remove the separately installed copy, then jailbreak again.

The touchscreen stopped responding after jailbreaking

ios.cfw.guide: on devices with a replacement screen, “especially if the screen is of low quality”, touch may not work after the userspace reboot. This is not a Dopamine bug. Force restart to return to stock iOS.

Sileo cannot refresh a source, or a package is “not found”

Check the URL exactly, including the trailing slash, and your connection. Packages are hidden when they do not match your layout (rootless) or your iOS version.

A banking or other app refuses to run

Expected on a jailbroken device. Use another device for it, or remove the jailbreak (Chapter 14). See Chapter 4 for why HCG does not cover bypasses.

Apple logo over and over, even after a force restart (bootloop)

Rare with a rootless, semi-untethered jailbreak, but possible. ios.cfw.guide: a bootloop “can be diagnosed by looking for a constant re-occuring Apple logo, even after you've forcefully rebooted”, and escaping it needs a restore in DFU mode. A restore erases the device and installs current iOS, ending the jailbreak; afterwards restore your backup. Ask for help first (r/jailbreak Discord, linked from ios.cfw.guide) and keep any crash logs.

Recovery Mode and DFU

Recovery Mode lets a computer reinstall iOS when the installed system cannot start. DFU mode is a deeper last resort that does not load iOS at all. Both are used by Finder or iTunes for restores, and both normally end with a fresh install of the newest signed iOS. Use them only when a force restart does not get you back.

Sources: ios.cfw.guide: Troubleshooting, FAQ; Dopamine 3.0.13 source (DOEnvironmentManager.m, jbdomain_watchdog.c, app strings); TrollStore README.

14

The way back

Removing a jailbreak

“Uninstalling a jailbreak isn't as simple as deleting the app” (ios.cfw.guide). Dopamine removes itself from inside its own app:

  1. Reboot into a non-jailbroken state. A normal restart does this.
  2. Open Dopamine and tap Settings.
  3. Choose Remove Jailbreak.
    • Not installed with TrollStore: leave Settings and tap the Remove Jailbreak button.
    • Installed with TrollStore: tap Continue. This may take a while; if it fails, start again from step 1.
  4. Reboot. Dopamine confirms “Successfully removed jailbreak. Rebooting the device is recommended.”
What it removes, and what it keeps

Dopamine: removing the jailbreak “will delete all jailbreak-related files, but all regular apps, files and data will be kept … You can't undo this action.” Every tweak, its settings and your sources are gone; re-jailbreaking starts from scratch.

Finish cleaning up

  • Delete the Dopamine app. Apps installed with TrollStore can only be removed from TrollStore itself (TrollStore README).
  • If you installed an update-blocking profile, remove it under Settings › General › VPN & Device Management. iOS can then update, which normally ends jailbreak support for this device.
  • Turn automatic updates back on, and install the latest iOS to get Apple's security fixes.
  • Before a repair or warranty visit, the most thorough option is to erase and restore the device with a computer, then restore your backup.

Moving to rootless palera1n? ios.cfw.guide says it is intercompatible with Dopamine, so removal is not required for that. Rootful palera1n is not intercompatible.

Sources: ios.cfw.guide: Removing Dopamine (page source last changed 23 Aug 2026); Dopamine 3.0.13 app strings; TrollStore README; Blocking Updates.

Glossary

36 terms

APT
The package system behind Sileo and Zebra. It downloads packages from repositories and resolves their dependencies.
Bootloop
The device keeps showing the Apple logo and never finishes starting, even after a force restart. Usually needs a restore.
Bootstrap
The base set of command-line tools and libraries a jailbreak installs. Dopamine uses Procursus.
Build number
Apple's exact identifier for an iOS release, e.g. 20H18 for 16.7 RC. Shown in Settings › General › About.
Chip (SoC)
The processor, such as A13 or M1. Jailbreak support depends on it. arm64 chips are A8–A11; arm64e chips are A12 and later.
Code signing
Apple's system for allowing only approved code to run. Sideloaded apps are signed with your own Apple Account for 7 days.
.deb
A package file: the software plus a description of its version, architecture and dependencies.
Depiction
A package's information page in a package manager or on a repository website.
Developer Mode
An iOS 16+ setting that must be on to run apps you sideloaded yourself.
DFU mode
Device Firmware Upgrade: a last-resort mode for restoring a device without loading iOS.
ElleKit
The tweak injection library Dopamine uses, by évelyne. It loads tweaks into apps and system processes.
Exploit
Code that takes advantage of a security flaw. Jailbreaks chain several to gain control of the kernel.
IPA / TIPA
An iOS app package. Dopamine is distributed as an .ipa; TrollStore also opens .tipa files.
Jailbreak
Using exploits to lift iOS restrictions on your own device so you can run unapproved software and modify the system.
Jetsam
The iOS feature that ends processes using too much memory. Dopamine's Jetsam Multiplier raises the limit; leave it alone unless advised.
Kernel
The core of the operating system that controls memory, hardware and permissions.
Package manager
An app such as Sileo or Zebra that installs, updates and removes jailbreak packages.
PAC, PPL, SPTM
Apple hardware and kernel protections (Pointer Authentication Codes, Page Protection Layer, Secure Page Table Monitor) that a jailbreak must bypass on newer chips.
Persistence Helper
A system app (often Tips) that TrollStore modifies so it can re-register TrollStore apps after iOS reloads its icon cache.
PreferenceLoader
A package that lets tweaks add their own pages to the Settings app.
Recovery Mode
A built-in mode in which a computer can reinstall iOS when the device cannot start normally.
Repository (repo, source)
A website that lists packages for package managers.
Respring
Restarting SpringBoard (the Home Screen and system interface). You stay jailbroken.
Rootful
An older jailbreak layout that writes into the system volume. Packages use iphoneos-arm.
Rootless
The layout Dopamine uses: all jailbreak files live under /var/jb and the system volume is left alone. Packages use iphoneos-arm64.
Roothide
A third layout with a randomised jailbreak root. Not used by Dopamine; packages use iphoneos-arm64e.
Safe Mode
A jailbroken state with tweaks turned off, used to remove a problem tweak.
Semi-untethered
A jailbreak that must be re-applied with an app on the device after every full reboot. Dopamine is one.
Sideloading
Installing an app from outside the App Store by signing it with your own Apple Account, using a computer tool such as PlumeImpactor.
Sileo
A package manager for jailbroken devices, and Dopamine's default suggestion.
SpringBoard
The iOS process that draws the Home Screen, Lock Screen and system interface.
TrollStore
An app that permanently installs other apps through a code-signing bug. Works only on 14.0 beta 2 – 16.6.1, 16.7 RC and 17.0.
Tweak
A small modification that changes how iOS or an app looks or behaves. Usually configured in Settings, not with its own icon.
Tweak injection
Loading tweaks into running apps and processes. Dopamine's Tweak Injection switch turns it on and off.
Userspace reboot
Restarting everything above the kernel without a full reboot. Dopamine and Sileo use it; you stay jailbroken.
/var/jb
The path where a rootless jailbreak keeps its files. It points to a hidden folder in /private/preboot.
